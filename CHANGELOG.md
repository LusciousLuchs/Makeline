# Makeline: what's new

## 0.3.0-alpha

- **Maximum immersion.** No top bar, no hints, no patience bars in first person. The time, your rating and the
  latest review are on a sign on the wall; customers' words appear as subtitles. A switch in the pause menu brings
  the helper HUD back.
- **The order terminal is on the register's monitor.** Take orders on a real-looking POS screen in the shop: size,
  crust, sauce, cheese, toppings, whole or half, light or extra, and Finish to send it.
- **Customers talk like people.** What they say is built on the spot from their mood, age, the time of day, how
  long you've kept them waiting and how many times you've made them repeat themselves, and the shop remembers what
  was said lately so the same line doesn't come round twice. Angry customers swear (a switch turns it off).
- **Slower, tenser pace.** Fewer orders, more patience, slower talking.
- **Pan pizzas come in small, medium and large.**
- **Satire notice and agreement.** A placeholder licence agreement, with a plain note that the game is satire,
  shows the first time you start it.
- Groundwork for multiplayer: every player action is now a command that can be sent and replayed.

## 0.2.0-alpha

- **Real shifts.** The store is open 9 AM to midnight, and your boss gives you a random 8-hour shift each day. You
  open if it starts at 9 and close if it ends at midnight. Tomorrow's shift is on the end-of-day screen.
- **Time of day matters.** Quiet mornings, a lunch rush, an afternoon lull, a big dinner rush, a late tail. The
  sky goes dark in the evening.
- **Empty shop, fast time.** When nobody is in and nothing is on the line, the game skips ahead until the next
  customer.

## 0.1.3-alpha

- **Over-topping is easy now.** Too little still gets noticed (under about 80% of a portion), but too much is
  forgiven up to about 1.6 times. Only a pile-on gets a complaint.
- **Thin crust is pre-made.** No tossing: you take a stamped, cracker-like crust from the box, and it bakes up
  crisp with docking holes and a flat edge. Sizes a crust doesn't come in are greyed out.

## 0.1.2-alpha

- **Slower clock.** The timing was too tight. A shop minute is now twelve real seconds, so an order is "late"
  after 4 real minutes, "very late" after 6 and "extreme" after 9. A shift is the 5:00 to 6:00 rush, about
  12 minutes.

## 0.1.1-alpha

- **New people.** Customers and staff are now properly proportioned, with faces of their own: head and jaw shape,
  eyes and eye colour, brows, nose, lips, ears, hair styles, beards, glasses, builds, heights and clothes. Their
  faces tighten and their arms fold as they lose patience. Staff wear the uniform and visor.
- Staff and applicants have real portraits in the manager's office.
- Better lighting, soft shadows under furniture and people, and more in the lobby (drinks fridge, table, tiled
  kitchen walls).

## 0.1.0-alpha

The first build.

- You're the manager of a pizza place, in first-person 3D. Walk the store: register, phone, makeline, oven, cut
  table, heat rack.
- Take orders at the counter and on the phone from customers who mumble, change their minds and hate repeating
  themselves. What you ring up is what gets made.
- Make pizzas by hand: 4 sizes, 3 crusts, 4 sauces, 6 cheeses, 12 toppings, whole or half, light / normal / extra.
  Portions matter.
- Cut (pie, or squares for thin crust), box, hand over.
- Every order has a timer: over 20 minutes is late, 30 very late, 45 extreme.
- Reviews build the store's score. Close a day under 3 stars and you're reprimanded; three in a week and you're fired.
- Hire customer service, makeline and drivers. They make mistakes, you can write them up, and unhappy staff quit.
- Deliveries run on a road map generated fresh each shift, out to 10 miles.
- The game updates itself from its GitHub releases.
